## Method notes

- Coordinates are projected exactly once (WGS84 -> EPSG:32639) and never fed to the renderer or physics as degrees.
- The national boundary is taken verbatim from the chosen source; the compiler never invents border geometry. No political claim is made about maritime boundaries: the water/airspace buffer is a gameplay area only.
- Maritime/territorial-water lines are deliberately NOT drawn, because no redistributable dataset was selected for them.
- Disputed or uncertain data is preserved as metadata rather than resolved.
